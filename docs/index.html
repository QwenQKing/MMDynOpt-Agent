Abstract

Recently, multimodal large language models have demonstrated strong potential in visual understanding and complex reasoning. However, they often struggle to transform multimodal evidence and question semantics into effective reasoning conditions. We propose MMDynOpt-Agent, which formulates dynamic multimodal reasoning optimization as a Markov decision process and trains a lightweight policy through end-to-end reinforcement learning. The agent interacts with a target MLLM as its environment, adaptively steering reasoning with multi-turn dynamic optimization prompts. A composite reward combines format compliance, answer correctness, and budget awareness to jointly improve accuracy and efficiency. The learned agent is transferable across target MLLMs and generalizes over fifteen public benchmarks.

Method

A lightweight policy observes the evolving interaction, generates a dynamic optimization prompt, and learns from outcome- and efficiency-aware rewards.
